# 0.2.0

Upgrading from 0.1.0. The image, its port and its environment variables are
those of 0.1.0; the changes are in the runtime configuration. See
[`CHANGELOG.md`](../CHANGELOG.md) for everything else.

## The platform top bar replaces the app grid

The top of the app is now the bar of Twake Workplace, which lists the apps
installed on the Workplace of the user.

- `appList.js`, `APP_GRID_AVAILABLE` and `app_dashboard.json` are no longer
  read: remove their mounts and keys (leaving them does no harm). Helm:
  remove `config.appList` and `config.appGridAvailable` from your values.
- For the menus of the platform (apps, help, account), the bar exchanges the
  ID token of the session on the Workplace of the user (OIDC mode only):
  - the Workplace is the `workplaceFqdn` claim of the SSO, else
    `WORKPLACE_FQDN_FALLBACK`;
  - the cozy-stack context lists the OIDC client of the app in
    `oidc.app_token_exchange` (`software_id: registry://mail`), and the `mail`
    app is installed on the instance;
  - `CSP_CONNECT_SRC` allows the Workplace origins (a wildcard such as
    `https://*.workplace.example.com` for per-user hosts).

  Without them (basic mode, no Workplace, an exchange refused) the bar shows
  the logotype and a log out button. Inside an iframe of the Workplace
  (`WORKPLACE_EMBEDDING`) the container shows the bar, as before.

See [`docs/deployment.md`](../docs/deployment.md#platform-top-bar).

## Team mailbox facade: `TWAKE_SPACE_URL` is no longer read

The facade of a team mailbox (`/embed/team-mailboxes/<id>`) learns the origin
of TwakeSpace from the greeting of the page that frames it
(`@linagora/twake-embed`). Keep TwakeSpace in `CSP_FRAME_ANCESTORS` (Helm:
`csp.frameAncestors`): it decides who may frame the app. Helm
`config.twakeSpaceUrl` only feeds `frame-ancestors`. See
[`docs/team-mailbox-embed.md`](../docs/team-mailbox-embed.md).

## New: `SENTRY_FEEDBACK_ENABLED`

`true` offers a "Send feedback" button to the users who opted in to error
reporting (Helm `config.sentry.feedbackEnabled`, off by default; Sentry 24.4.2
or later). It is also read from the container environment when the
configuration comes from an `env.file`. See [`docs/sentry.md`](../docs/sentry.md).
